Privacy policy

Worky · operated by pk1code · updated 13 October 2026

This page is the privacy policy for the Worky Android app (com.lifestations.app). The terms are a separate page.

Information we keep

We keep the Google account email, the WhatsApp number, the language you choose, the place you save, and the posts and files you upload. We also keep Worky messages, voice notes, and the push token used to notify the other person.

We keep this so you can sign in, show a post, be contacted, and get a notification when someone messages you.

Who can see it

People who browse Worky can see an active post, its photo, video, or voice, and that it is about 2 km away, not the exact place. Each person can open WhatsApp or a phone call from posts only 3 times a day. The app does not give them your email. From Mine you can stop new contact on a post, or block one number. You can also pause or delete a post.

Worky

From a post, a person can message you in Worky. A message is text or a short voice note. Both people in the thread can read it. Either person can block. A block closes the thread for both people.

Google

Google Firebase stores this for us and provides sign-in. Firebase Analytics may use a device identifier or advertising id to count how the app is used. The app does not show third-party ads. We do not sell your information.

Children

Worky is not for children under 13. Do not use the app if you are under 13.

Delete your account

The steps are on the delete account page.

In the app, open Mine. Tap the red icon of a person with a line through it, then tap it again. That deletes your posts, your uploaded files, your Worky messages and voice notes, your profile, and your sign-in. It does not delete copies someone else already saved on their own phone.

If you already uninstalled the app, email customercare@kambazaar.org from the Google account you used in Worky and ask us to delete the account. We delete the same records. Analytics already sent to Google may remain under Google’s own rules. We keep the deletion email only long enough to finish the request.
