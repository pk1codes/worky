Terms and privacy

Worky · operated by pk1code · updated 10 October 2026

This page is the terms and the privacy policy for the Worky Android app (com.lifestations.app). It describes only what the app does.

Using Worky

You sign in with Google. You add a WhatsApp number. You can post your own photo, video, or voice, with a place, so nearby people can find the post.

Post only material that is yours. Do not post anything illegal, or anything that harms another person. You can pause or delete your own posts in the app.

Information we keep

We keep the Google account email, the WhatsApp number, the language you choose, the place you save, and the posts and files you upload. We also keep Worky Talk messages, voice notes, and the push token used to notify the other person. Google Firebase stores this for us and provides sign-in. Firebase Analytics may use a device identifier or advertising id to count how the app is used. The app does not show third-party ads. We do not sell your information.

Who can see it

People who browse Worky can see an active post, its photo, video, or voice, and that it is about 2 km away, not the exact place. Each person can open WhatsApp or a phone call from posts only 3 times a day. The app does not give them your email. From Mine you can stop new contact on a post, or block one number. You can also pause or delete a post.

Worky Talk

From a post, a person can message you in Worky Talk. A message is text or a short voice note. Both people in the thread can read it. A video button starts a Google Meet on the caller's own Google account. Worky does not record the call. The other phone gets a notification, and that notification does not contain the meeting link. The other person joins from the thread. Either person can block. A block closes the thread for both people.

Children

Worky is not for children under 13. Do not use the app if you are under 13.

Delete your account

In the app, open Mine. Tap the red icon of a person with a line through it, then tap it again. That deletes your posts, your uploaded files, your Worky Talk messages and voice notes, your profile, and your sign-in. It does not delete copies someone else already saved on their own phone. It does not delete a Google Meet from the caller's calendar.

If you already uninstalled the app, email customercare@kambazaar.org from the Google account you used in Worky and ask us to delete the account. We delete the same records. Analytics already sent to Google may remain under Google’s own rules. We keep the deletion email only long enough to finish the request.
