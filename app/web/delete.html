Delete your account

Worky · operated by pk1code · updated 11 October 2026

This page is how you delete your account in the Worky Android app (com.lifestations.app). The privacy policy and the terms are separate pages.

In the app

Open Mine. Tap the red icon of a person with a line through it. Tap it again.

What is deleted

Your posts. Your uploaded files. Your Worky Talk messages and voice notes. Your profile. Your sign-in.

What is kept

Copies someone else already saved on their own phone. The Worky Talk event on the caller's Google Calendar. Analytics already sent to Google stay under Google's own rules.

If the app is gone

Email customercare@kambazaar.org from the Google account you used in Worky. Ask us to delete the account. We delete the same records. We keep that email only until the deletion is finished.
